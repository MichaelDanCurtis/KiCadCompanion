# BL-13 — Complete human-assisted physical acceptance

Status: proposed; not activated. Phase: Validation.
Prerequisites: BL-12. Read [the shared execution contract](../GOALS.md) before starting.

## Outcome

Record a successful physical prototype bring-up and enclosure fit test against an owner-approved test protocol, tied to the exact software-verified revisions.

## Scope and reuse

Only the explicitly approved physical unit, hardware actions, instruments, and test plan. Human assembly and measurements are required; Codex coordinates and records, not fabricates observations.

Reuse first: BL-12 release manifest, bring-up checklist, firmware identity/logs, FreeCAD dimensions, BOM/source records.

## Deliverables

- Approved per-unit test protocol with expected values/tolerances, stop conditions, equipment, device identity, and operator.
- Evidence of actual assembly/print fit, connector access, safe power-up, firmware behavior, and frontend/device interaction when applicable.
- Failure/rework records and final requirement-to-measurement matrix.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **BL-13-A1** — Owner supplies or authorizes the exact unit, flash action, power/test limits, and required equipment before live writes or energization.
- [ ] **BL-13-A2** — Printed enclosure and actual PCB/module assembly are physically checked for fit, fastener/cable access, connector alignment, and relevant antenna/thermal constraints.
- [ ] **BL-13-A3** — Firmware identity and board revision are recorded; functional tests use measured/logged responses with timestamps and operator confirmation.
- [ ] **BL-13-A4** — Every required criterion passes on the final revision, or the goal remains incomplete with failures and required redesign explicitly recorded.
- [ ] **BL-13-A5** — Human reports/photos/logs are identified as such; simulated values and inferred outcomes never substitute for measurements.

## Verification

Owner-approved physical procedure, serial/instrument logs and operator-confirmed fit evidence mapped to acceptance IDs. Capture uncertainties and repeat tests affected by rework.

Record each criterion in [the evidence record](../evidence/BL-13.md) using the shared template. That record is created when work starts; absence means no evidence yet. Record commands, exit codes, tool versions, input/output revisions, before/after observations, and any manual test steps. A baseline failure remains a finding, not an excuse to delete or skip a required test.

## Blockers and authority

No hardware, no approved flash/power plan, missing measurement equipment, or unresolved failure. Do not spin while awaiting physical work; report the next human action and use host-controlled goal pause/resume.

## Non-goals

No purchases or printer submission without approval, mains/high-voltage testing, certification, destructive provisioning, or invented physical evidence.

## Completion handoff

Report: criteria passed/failed/not run, evidence links, exact changed files, remaining risks, and whether the next goal's prerequisites are now met. Update the local roadmap status only with evidence. Stop after this goal; starting the next goal is the user's decision.
